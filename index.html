Policies & Guidelines

Attendance & Registration ＋
Academy players are automatically expected to attend. Notify by 2:00 PM Thursday or 10:00 PM Friday for Saturday.

Training registration is required at least 2 hours before sessions.

Maximum 2 walk-ins per calendar month; the 3rd onward is charged at 1.5×.
Payment ＋
Payment must be completed within 12 hours after the session ends.

Payments still unpaid by 5:00 PM the following day incur a flat ₱100 overdue penalty.
Packages & Cancellations ＋
Packages are non-transferable and non-refundable.

If weather cancellation occurs within the first 45 minutes, the session is not deducted. If training runs more than 45 minutes, it counts as completed.
